# dM2008-Alexandra-Koh-AY2526-S1

## Mini Project - Final Project Reflection

I had no ideas for this project and referred to the first couple of weeks slides. George Nees projects caught my eye, the breaking of a structure. I was enticed by the challenge, as I had just gotten the grasp of creating grids. Combining that notion and the learnings of ultrasonic sensor, it inspired my idea of how earth's structure declined as humans discovered more and more. 

I embarked on the project with regular rectangles and for loops to create a grid. I used a class to create each square and update it. The passing of values from ultrasonic sensor, to arduino to p5js took longer than anticipated. I created a function to convert the distance to the amount of affected area. By then, I had a prototype of fallling squares if the affected amount fulfiled the conditions.

To create more intererst, I then replaced the square with lines to increase the flexibility of the breakage. I wanted the points of the lines to influence each other and managed it with the help of copilot in the storing of previous locations of each point.

The restoring factor was needed to show structure's resilience and ability to recover when left alone. After a certain distance, when the distance is far enough, the grid was to be recalled. I tried looping at first til I found exponential easing with ai, and it turned out quite mesmerising. 

Overall, I found this project eye opening to the possibilities of interactive art, from others creative projects as well.
